F1 ADVERTISING SPACE ANALYSIS

Billions of dollars are spent on advertising during F1 races. This project studies which parts of the car receive the most exposure across race broadcasts and other F1 media. The image below shows 2014 estimates for the advertising price for various placements.

Skills and Tools

  • PyTorch (convolutional neural networks)
  • Python and yt-dlp for YouTube frame collection
  • R (ggplot2)

Results

While the 2014 pricing estimates claim that the side and rear of the car are the most valuable by far, our data shows lots of visibility for the front and inside angles as well.

Additionally, shorter content showed the inside of the car (not even considered for 2014 advert pricing) more often and the rear of the car less often. This placements can be used to target younger audiences through social media, while rear-of-car placements may better target dedicated race enthusiasts.

Camera-angle distributions varied by track, but the differences were not consistent enough to support specific recommendations.

Model Accuracy

The final model reached approximately 85% validation accuracy and identified the inside or cockpit camera angle correctly more than 97% of the time. Much of the remaining inaccuracy came from subjective labels, such as deciding whether a car was distant.

Step-by-Step

Data collection

  1. Downloaded F1 race highlights with extract_frame.py and yt-dlp.
  2. Manually labeled frames with label.py.
  3. Sorted and balanced the images into the structure expected by PyTorch's ImageFolder dataset.
  4. Augmented the training data with cropping and noise scripts.

Modeling

Several architectures were tested, including a two-level classification model. Training metrics used a separate validation set to reduce the risk of overfitting. The final model used ResNet-18 initialized with pre-existing weights.

For frame-by-frame classification, mode filtering smoothed prediction noise. This made it possible to analyze camera-angle patterns across individual frames and races.

Future Development

  • Improve labeling consistency and expand the dataset with more races and camera angles.
  • Analyze exposure by specific car region and advertising placement.
  • Compare camera-angle distributions across more tracks and race formats.
  • Evaluate how content platform and video length affect advertising exposure.
